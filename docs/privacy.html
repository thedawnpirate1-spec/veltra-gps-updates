Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Controller

Leon Lammerich, Gotenweg 24, 53859 Niederkassel, Germany, veltragps@gmail.com

2. The app works locally

No telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, no user account. Input from keyboard, mouse, joystick or controller is processed in memory only and is never stored or sent.

3. Data stored on your PC

Profiles and settings, log files (deleted after 14 days), the licence key (encrypted with Windows DPAPI) and, while tweaks are active, the original Windows values so they can be restored exactly. None of this leaves your PC. You can delete it in the app.

4. Licence check (Lemon Squeezy)

On activation and at most once a day, the app sends the licence key, a pseudonymous device name (a hash without user or computer name) and the activation ID to api.lemonsqueezy.com. The server also sees your IP address and the time. Purpose: checking that the licence is valid (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Lemon Squeezy is the Merchant of Record and processes order and payment data under its own privacy policy. Transfer to the USA: EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR), as set out in the Lemon Squeezy DPA.

5. Network test

Only when you click it: 20 pings each to your router, 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare) and 8.8.8.8 (Google). No personal data is sent; those services see your IP address and the time, as with any ping.

6. This website

Hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub may log your IP address for security. This site uses no cookies and no tracking.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us at the address above.
